VUEO — Android TV Launcher Channels

Base: repo (61) with the previous rating/Watched patches applied.
This ZIP only changes TV launcher integration. Extract at repository root and replace.
No YML; no APK; build TV release after applying.

Creates two stable Preview Channels: Watchlist (default), For You (optional).
Requests default visibility only when Watchlist is first created; later sync does not force a hidden channel back on.
Enable For You through Android TV Customize channels. Launcher behavior depends on the device.
Adds the required TV-provider permission and INITIALIZE_PROGRAMS receiver.
Uses existing Watchlist and already-received Home For You results: no stream scan or recommendation API request.
At most 24 programs per channel. Programs update in place; stale/profile-old entries are removed.
For You snapshot is profile-scoped, respects kids certification and personalization settings, expires after 24 hours.
When visiting Home, feed updates refresh the snapshot. No periodic background network work.
Refresh also occurs at app startup, library/profile changes and when leaving the app.
Cards open Details; logo opens Library or Home. Cross-profile stale card links are ignored.
The existing Watch Next path remains separate.

After installing: open VUEO, select profile and let Home load, then return to launcher.
Check Customize channels -> Vueo. Save at least one Watchlist title and obtain For You suggestions to populate rows.
Empty rows may not be rendered by the launcher. Additional channel visibility is controlled by the user/system.
