Changes
1. Provider-scoped in-memory cookie session
   - Native fetch and axios requests from one provider share cookies.
   - Cookies do not leak between provider executions.
   - Matching cookies are copied to a returned playable stream when the provider did not supply a Cookie header.
2. Resolver output normalization
   - Accepts arrays, streams/sources/results/items/data/links wrappers, single stream/result objects, and direct stream objects.
3. Stream metadata normalization
   - URL aliases: url, streamUrl, playbackUrl, link, src, file.
   - Torrent aliases: infoHash, hash, btih, magnet, plus file index.
   - Quality, codec, HDR, audio, language, size, rank, and nested proxy/request headers.
