# TV 30A — Premium Home Density

Home keeps the locked 29C.4 floating sidebar architecture. This pass changes presentation only.

- Hero reading zone targets roughly the upper 50% of the TV viewport.
- Continue Watching stays medium landscape (224dp, 16:9) with title/meta over artwork and a 3dp progress line.
- My List and catalog rails use medium 2:3 posters (128dp), giving roughly 5–6 visible posters on a common TV viewport.
- Focus scale is restrained at ~1.028 with a shallow neutral edge/shadow.
- Rail spacing is tighter so the screen shows more content without looking cramped.
- Backdrop scrims are lighter so artwork reads as cinematic rather than buried under black.
- Sidebar focus/return behavior and all Home data/navigation contracts are unchanged.
